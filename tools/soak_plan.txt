# The desktop build's soak: one round through the game's scenes in under 3 minutes with the boot, for the crashes and
# hangs a short boot misses (the ops checklist's 8d), played by tools/frame_dump.mjs --soak 180 on the Linux build. A
# round plays the boot (the logo, the title's Mario head), Start to the file select, file A with A on a new save, its
# intro (Peach's letter, Lakitu, the pipe) passed with A until Mario stands in the castle grounds, then 10 s of random
# input there (the stick, A, B, Z and the camera's C buttons). patches/shader_survey.c then warps Mario into the castle
# as its door does, for 10 s of random input in the lobby, its first entry's dialog passed with A, and into Bob-omb
# Battlefield as its painting does, its act select taken with A, where he plays 20 s of random input, pauses and
# resumes, and 10 s more. Last, Jolly Roger Bay, a course of water, the same way for 15 s. Mario is kept at full health
# (the survey's invincible) so no death ends a course early. Each scene is held to the game by until steps on the
# survey's struct, found by its magic words: survey+0x20 the level playing (16 the grounds, 6 the castle, 9 Bob-omb
# Battlefield, 12 Jolly Roger Bay) and survey+0x2C Mario's action (0x0C400201, standing idle: in control, no cutscene or
# dialog). The file select is known by its cursor on file A (the decomp's sCursorPos, -94.0 at 0x801A7D1C). Start is
# never pressed at random: its pause menu's options leave the course. The keys are the game's default keyboard bindings:
# W A S D the stick, Space A, Shift B, Q Z, Enter Start, the arrows the C buttons.
